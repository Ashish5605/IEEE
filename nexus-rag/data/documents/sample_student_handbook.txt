Student Handbook — Attendance and Leave Policy (Sample)

Section 1: Attendance Requirement
Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each course to be eligible to sit for the semester examinations. Attendance is calculated based on the number of classes conducted versus the number of classes attended.

Section 2: Attendance Shortfall
Students whose attendance falls below 75% but is at or above 65% may apply for condonation through the Head of Department, subject to valid medical or personal grounds supported by documentation. Students below 65% attendance will not be permitted to write the semester examination and must repeat the course.

Section 3: Leave Types
There are two types of leave available to students: Medical Leave and On-Duty Leave. Medical Leave requires a certificate from a registered medical practitioner submitted within 3 working days of returning to college. On-Duty Leave is granted for participation in college-approved events such as symposiums, sports, and NSS activities, and must be applied for at least 3 days in advance through the faculty advisor.

Section 4: Examination Rules
Students must carry their college ID card to all examinations. Use of mobile phones or any electronic device in the examination hall is strictly prohibited and will be treated as a case of malpractice. Students arriving more than 30 minutes after the start of an examination will not be permitted to enter the hall.
